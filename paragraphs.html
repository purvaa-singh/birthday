Words for you

Some paragraphs written by your loving boyfriend.
Bohot sara kuch kuch likha hai maine

01

Thank you for being you

Kabhi socha nahi tha ki life itna beautiful full circle moment degi… pehle dost thi, phir beech mein sab khamosh ho gaya. Aur phir achanak tu wapas aayi… (tereko meri value pata chali )aur iss baar sirf dost banke nahi, meri life ka sabse important hissa ban kar. Thank you for being you — itni real, itni pure, itni special. Tere saath har baat easy lagti hai, har din thoda better ho jata hai. Shayad hum alag hone ke baad hi samajh paaye ki hum ek dusre ke liye kitne important hain. Ab jab tu mere paas hai, bas yahi feel hota hai ki finally sab sahi jagah aa gaya hai. Tu meri kahani ka best part hai… aur honestly, main tereko phir kabhi lose nahi karna chahta.

02

Don't ever change

Tu jaisi hai, bilkul perfect hai. Teri hasi, teri baatein, tera care karne ka tarika… sab kuch itna special hai ki main kabhi change nahi chahta. Teri simplicity hi teri sabse badi khoobsurati hai. Bas aise hi rehna real, honest, aur apne dil ki sunne wali. Main hamesha tere saath hoon, chahe kuch bhi ho. Teri har khushi, har sapna, har emotion mere liye important hai. Don't ever change for anyone… because tu jaisi hai, wahi best hai.

03

Love you chotu

Aur chotu sun… ab bas main hi hoon teri life mein matlab itna zaroor ki tu kabhi akeli feel na kare, main hamesha tere saath rahun. Mere alawa kisi aur ko dekhegi bhi na toh thoda jealous toh ho jaunga, par woh bhi sirf isliye kyunki tu meri hai aur mujhe farak padta hai. Aur breakup wagairah ka toh galti se bhi mat sochna… kyunki main waise hi nahi jaane wala itni easily. Par sach yeh hai ki main tujhe rokunga zabardasti se nahi, balki itna pyaar dunga ki tujhe kabhi jaane ka mann hi na kare. Tu meri chotu hai, aur main tera banda aur yeh jo hum hai na, main ise sambhaal ke rakhunga, har haal mein.

04

Babyy Gurll

Chotu… kabhi kabhi lagta hai ki main kitna lucky hoon ki tu meri life mein hai. Tu sirf meri girlfriend nahi hai, tu meri comfort hai, meri habit hai, meri har roz ki khushi hai. Din ka best part tu hai, aur raat ka last thought bhi tu hi hoti hai. Mujhe tere saath har chhoti si cheez special lagti hai — teri hasi, teri awaaz, teri stupid si baatein bhi. Tu paas ho ya door, tu mere dimaag se jaati hi nahi. Aur honestly, main jaana bhi nahi chahta. Main bas tujhse itna pyaar karta hoon ki kabhi kabhi words bhi kam pad jaate hain. Tu meri “chotu” hai, meri peace hai, meri favourite feeling hai. Aur main bas yeh chahta hoon ki tu hamesha meri life mein rahe… kyunki tere bina sab kuch thoda sa adhoora lagta hai.
